📊 Zomato Restaurant Expansion Strategy

**Problem Statement**

I was tasked with identifying data-driven strategies for expanding Zomato’s restaurant presence in new locations. The goal of this project was to analyze existing restaurant data to uncover insights and provide actionable recommendations for opening new restaurants in areas with high potential for growth and profitability.

**Objectives**

Analyze key factors influencing restaurant success

Identify high-demand areas with fewer existing restaurants

Understand customer preferences and cuisine popularity

Recommend optimal locations for new restaurant openings

Support expansion decisions with data-driven insights

**Tools Used**

Microsoft Excel

Google Spreadsheets

**Key Insights & Recommendations**

Identified underserved locations with strong customer demand

Analyzed cuisine trends and top-rated restaurant types

Recommended specific areas for expansion based on data patterns

Suggested strategies to minimize competition and maximize visibility

**Outcome**

The insights from this analysis provide a strategic foundation for Zomato to make informed decisions regarding restaurant expansion, helping improve customer reach and business growth.
